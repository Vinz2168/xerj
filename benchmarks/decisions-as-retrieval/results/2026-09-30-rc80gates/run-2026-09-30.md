## Gate 1 — #1057 tier-2 zero-shot quality. Verdict: **FAIL** (all bars)

Gate text: "zero-shot SMS accuracy >=0.95 and ECE <=0.05, AG News >=0.85, Banking77
reported not gated; below the bar it ships behind a flag with the numbers published."

What the "zero-shot head" is, determined from #1057/#1072 + the binary: there is
**no downloaded pretrained model** — `--decide-mode local` has no download path and no
egress (`engine/crates/xerj-ai/src/decide.rs` "no hub download"); it loads whatever NLI
model directory `--decide-model-dir` names. The only release artifact is the trained
`xerj-decide-v1` (#1064), scored here through the node's zero-shot answering mode
(labels arrive per request; tier 2 = no history). AG News is the genuinely zero-shot
dataset (never in the artifact's training); SMS and Banking77 are in its training
corpus. Scored on the feature build, no `[decisions]` index, every answer tier `local`.

| dataset | n | accuracy | ECE | conf≥0.8 | acc@≥0.8 | ms/item | bar | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| SMS held-out, noul (`/_decide`, positive_label=spam) | 1,574 | **0.9193** | **0.287** (p_raw-based: 0.3439) | 18.11% | 0.8035 | 14.6 | acc ≥0.95, ECE ≤0.05 | **FAIL / FAIL** |
| AG News test, 4-way choice (`/v1/systemone`) | 7,600 | **0.2599** | 0.060 | 0.00% | — | 52.8 | ≥0.85 | **FAIL** (chance = 0.25) |
| Banking77 test, 77-way choice (`/v1/systemone`) | 3,080 | **0.1185** | 0.0717 | 0.00% | — | 376.1 | reported, not gated | reported |

SMS spam P/R/F1 through the node: 0.643 / 0.982 / 0.778. The rule for a failed
gate — "ships behind a flag with the numbers published" — is doubly true here: the
flag is the `decide-local` cargo feature, which stock release builds do not enable
(see run header), i.e. in the shipped binary tier 2 is absent, not merely flagged off.

Cross-check vs the model card (`docs/DECIDE_MODEL.md`, same artifact, same rows,
in-harness `Head::score`): Banking77 reproduces **exactly** (0.1185 / ECE 0.072 /
0% at 0.8 — the choice path renormalises identically). SMS differs: card 0.8850 /
ECE 0.310 vs node 0.9193 / 0.287. Verified on the wire: for a noul the two
hypothesis scores sum to 1 and `confidence = max(p_raw, 1−p_raw)` (1,574/1,574 rows),
so the node's argmax-over-pair is a slightly different decision rule than the card's
eval; the borderline rows differ. Both numbers are published; the release-path number
is the node one.

## Gate 2 — #1057 latency + download ACs

AC: "p50 ≤ 50 ms for 30 questions on 8 CPU cores; quantized weights, download ≤ 300 MB".

Method: node booted under `taskset -c 0-7` (verified: `pid 1339286's current
affinity list: 0-7`), 30 distinct questions per shape, single sequential client,
wall-clock, warm-up excluded (reported as cold), loadavg 2.2–3.1 (co-tenant agents
active — upper-bound-ish, not a quiet-machine number).

| shape | cold | p50 | p99 | mean | verdict vs 50 ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `POST /_decide`, no index, noul (2 hypotheses) | 40.1 ms | **8.4 ms** | 14.2 ms | 8.9 ms | **PASS** |
| `/v1/systemone`, one noul | 13.3 ms | **8.4 ms** | 14.2 ms | 9.0 ms | **PASS** |
| `/v1/systemone`, 5-option choice | 28.7 ms | **20.0 ms** | 32.3 ms | 20.4 ms | **PASS** |
| `/v1/systemone`, 77-option choice | 509.7 ms | 282.6 ms | 549.0 ms | 317.0 ms | over bar (context) |

Weights/download: the tier downloads **0 MB** — there is no download path
(`--decide-model-dir` is a local directory; no hf-hub, no egress). The artifact on
disk is **28.5 MiB** (29,883,831 bytes: `model.safetensors` 29,683,844 +
`tokenizer.json` 189,846 + `config.json` 641 + manifest/version) — far under the
300 MB budget. It is **F32, not quantized** (candle 0.9 has no quantized
safetensors `VarBuilder`; PR #1072 documents this as deliberate). AC met on size
and latency; "quantized" is not met and was never buildable on this loader.

## Gate 4 — #1061 flywheel replay. Verdict: **FAIL** (both arms miss a bar)

Gate text: "Banking77 replay, after 2,000 cached hosted answers, >=80% of later
traffic answered by history at >=0.8 confidence with accuracy >=0.97."

Tier 3 (hosted) is not built, so the replay runs twice: arm a is the ladder exactly
as shipped (the node's own tier-2 answers are the cached ones — the only answers the
flywheel can actually cache today); arm b instantiates the gate's premise (2,000
gold answers cached in the write-back's own document shape, `source="hosted"`).
Both use eval.py's seed-11 shuffle of the 3,080 test rows (the file is label-sorted;
an unshuffled head/tail split measures label ordering, not the flywheel — the first,
unshuffled run is preserved in `replay1061.history-sorted-run.out` and showed
accuracy 0.0 by construction).

**Arm a — live flywheel (as shipped).** 2,000 fill requests fired; only **12**
tier-2 answers were ever cached (write-back only fires when tier 2 answers, and
after the first cached doc the history vote returns support for ~99.4% of traffic —
a BM25 `match` hit on any cached document counts as support). The last fill request
tier 2 answered was #1,684; the cache asymptotes at 12 docs. The gate's premise
("after 2,000 cached answers") is **unreachable through the ladder itself** on
Banking77-shaped traffic. Later traffic (the other 1,080 rows):

| metric | value | bar | verdict |
|---|---:|---|---|
| share answered by history (any conf) | 99.72% | — | — |
| share answered by history at conf ≥0.8 | **4.91%** | ≥80% | **FAIL** |
| accuracy of that share | **0.0377** | ≥0.97 | **FAIL** |
| accuracy of all history answers | 0.0334 | — | — |
| overall accuracy on later traffic | 0.0333 | — | — |

Reading: the flywheel locks in a tiny, mostly-wrong cache and then answers
essentially everything from it — the measured mechanism failure is the freeze plus
tier-2's own 0.1185 Banking77 accuracy compounding into 3.3% accuracy on later
traffic.

**Arm b — the gate's premise (2,000 gold answers cached, shuffled identically).**

| metric | value | bar | verdict |
|---|---:|---|---|
| share of later traffic (1,080 rows) answered by history at conf ≥0.8 | **19.72%** | ≥80% | **FAIL** (4× under) |
| accuracy of that share | **0.9953** | ≥0.97 | **PASS** |
| overall history accuracy | 0.7009 | — | — |

Reading: with a correct 2,000-answer cache, the vote's confident slice is easily
accurate enough, but a 2,000-doc history only clears confidence 0.8 on ~20% of
traffic (the vote's published 50.6%-at-0.8 coverage was with the full 10,003-doc
train history; the blog's 87% basis likewise). The 80% share bar needs a far larger
cache than 2,000 answers at k=10 — or the hybrid/minilm neighbour arms.

  (loadavg 2–11); the latency node was pinned to cores 0-7 but the client was not.

  `a179e1ef3`, not the stock release binary (which cannot run tier 2 — run header).

  `p` is not used by the vote, so the substitution is exact for the vote's inputs.
